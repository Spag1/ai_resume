# Prompt: draw the 17 Enhancv templates as finished CVs

Paste everything below the line to the model that draws the pages. Pixel sizes, fonts, and color pairs are in [enhancv-template-reproduction.md](enhancv-template-reproduction.md). This prompt supplies the page rules that prompt missed, and the dummy resume each template must contain.

---

You are drawing 17 finished, one-page CVs. Each page is a resume a person could send to an employer. It is not a wireframe, not a template-picker card, and not a schematic.

The last attempt failed in two ways. Fix both.

1. The pages did not look like resumes. They showed “YOUR NAME”, a title bar, and a few gray lines. A CV has a real name, a real target title, a summary paragraph, two or three jobs with several bullets each, education, and the side sections listed below. Use the dummy text in this prompt. Do not shorten a job to one line. Do not invent “Title / Company / 2010–2014”. Do not draw gray placeholder bars.

2. The colored ribbon was not full page. A sidebar is a vertical band from the top edge of the paper to the bottom edge. A header band is a horizontal band from the left edge of the paper to the right edge. Neither is a small colored rectangle floating inside white margins.

## How to draw the page

- One A4 page per template, portrait, 210 × 297 mm. Fill the page. Do not float a small resume inside a card, and do not return a gallery of thumbnails as the result. If you also draw a picker, each thumbnail is a scaled copy of the finished CV, not a new schematic.
- White page, except where a template paints a band, a sidebar, or a page background. Those paints touch the edge of the paper.
- Body text is near-black (#3e3e3e), about 12–13 px. Section labels are uppercase, in the heading font, in the primary color, with a rule under them, except where a template below says otherwise.
- Job title in the primary color. Employer in the accent color. Dates and city in the body color, with a small calendar icon and a small pin icon, except Classic, Crest, Imprint, and Minimal, which have no icons.
- Footer on every page: “www.enhancv.com” at the lower left, “Powered by Enhancv” at the lower right.
- Where a template has a photo, draw a circular headshot. Where it says no photo, leave the photo out.
- Keep each CV to one page. Use the sections in the order given. Do not add sections that are not listed.

## The ribbon rule, per template

- **Creative.** The navy band (#14324c) is the full width of the paper, edge to edge, about 200 px tall (240 px because this one has a photo). There is no white margin above it or at its sides. The name, the title, and the contacts are white and sit inside the band. The circular photo sits inside the band, on the right. Below the band the page is white and splits into two columns.
- **Elegant.** The navy sidebar (#22405C) is the full height of the paper, on the right, about 32% of the width. It starts at the top edge and ends at the bottom edge. A circular photo is the first thing in that sidebar. Sidebar text is white. The left side is white and holds the name and the story.
- **Polished.** The dark teal sidebar (#006666) is the full height of the paper, on the left, about 32% of the width, top edge to bottom edge. The name is white and sits at the top of that sidebar. There is no photo. The right side is white and starts with the colored title, not with the name.
- **Contemporary.** The left rail is full height. It is white, with pale green blobs behind a large circular photo at the top. It is not a short green sticker. The name is on the white right side, stacked on two lines, very large. The title sits in a green pill under the name.
- **Compact.** The pale blue decorative background covers the whole page, corner to corner, with soft shapes in the corners. The text sits on that background. It is not a white card with a blue border.
- **Crest.** The cream background covers the whole page, edge to edge.

Every other template is white to the edges, with a header that spans the content width and columns underneath.
